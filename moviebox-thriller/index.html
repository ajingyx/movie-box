Your MovieBox selection method

CASE 01 / Puzzle

Choose a layered mystery when connecting details is part of the pleasure. Avoid plot summaries that reveal later turns.

CASE 02 / Espionage

Try a spy story if you enjoy conflicting motives, patient scenes and trust that changes from one conversation to the next.

CASE 03 / Pursuit

Pick an action-led investigation when you want a strong objective and forward movement. Check the synopsis for your preferred intensity.
